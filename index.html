Role Explorer

Search for a role to see its stage-by-stage numbers: how many were called at each phase, what share of the original pool that is, and how many of the previous stage's candidates actually carried through to the next one — matched by name, not just batch counts divided against each other. When a stage spans more than one posting (re-announcements, multiple batches), that's called out, since dividing raw totals across separate batches can be misleading.
